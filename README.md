# Nova for Mac

A little star that lives in your MacBook's notch. It keeps time on your focus and breaks, greets you, and taps you when a meeting is coming or an AI agent needs you.

**[Download Nova](https://github.com/Yarielito06/nova-releases/releases/latest/download/Nova.dmg)** · [Website](https://yarielito06.github.io/nova-releases/)

Open the file, drag Nova into Applications, and open it. Nova is signed and notarized by Apple. For MacBooks with a notch, macOS 14 or later.

This repository only hosts Nova's downloads and website.
